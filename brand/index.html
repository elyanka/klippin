01

Introduction

Klippin runs one infrastructure with two equal systems: Client for the brands that pay for distribution, and Clipper for the creators who clip. They share one logo family, one shared core (status colours, spacing, radius, icons, voice) and one token file. Every system chapter has the same sections on both sides; switch to read the other.

Archetypes

Enterprise-facing. Scale, control and proof.

Magician50%

High-contrast cool palettes and the K's cutting blade signal creation at scale. Cyan and Bright Sky Blue are kept for creative highlights.

Variants

Four core variants. The entertainment wordmark may add symbol accents when context calls for them; see Color Strategy.

Entertainment wordmarkPlus and sound wave sit inside the Ps. Use the plain treatment by default; the symbol accents may be added when they aid the entertainment context.
Simple wordmarkRegular Ps. The default name lockup for interfaces and tight spaces, and the fallback below 240 px wide.
MonogramThe K alone, with an accent triangle. For when the brand is already known: app icons, avatars, social, merch. Works at any size, including large.

Dark · WhiteDefault. White letters, accent gradient details. #050B22.
