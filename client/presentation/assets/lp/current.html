How it works

Three steps. That's the whole thing.

No application to write, no follower count to hit. The clips you already know how to make, pointed at campaigns that pay.

STEP 01

Install the app

Sign up in under a minute and connect the accounts you post from. Nothing to pay, nothing to prove upfront.

STEP 02

Join a campaign

Browse what's live, pick the ones you want. Each campaign shows what it pays, what it needs, and the assets to clip from. Some are open to everyone, the biggest ones open on approval.

STEP 03

Post and earn

Publish on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Views are verified, then they count. Follow every clip and every dollar in the app, from the moment you post.

Earnings

What you can actually make.

Where you land depends on how much you post and how far the clips travel. Here is what that looks like in practice.

Month 1 & 2

Beginner

$300 to $1,000/ month

You learn how campaigns work, you test formats, and you find out what actually performs. Most of this stage is discovering which of your clips travel, and why.

Month 3 to 6

Intermediate

$1,000 to $4,000/ month

The formats stop being guesswork. You post more often, you know which angles land, and you start getting into the campaigns that pay more.

6+ months

Advanced

Unlimited

Our best clippers clear $12K+ a month, producing at scale across several campaigns at once. There is no ceiling here: you are paid per verified view, and nobody caps how many you bring in.

These are real figures observed on the Klippin network. They are not estimates, and they are not a promise of income: what you earn depends on how much you post and on how your clips perform.

Most start at the first one. Nobody has to stay there.

What matters is the clip. That is also why you can post the same campaign from several accounts.

A phone or a computer, a free editing app, and time. Nothing else. You do not need a camera, you do not need paid software, and you do not need an audience.

You never pay anything either: joining the network is free, joining a campaign is free, and Klippin never takes money from a clipper.

You are paid per thousand verified views. Each campaign sets its own rate and shows it before you join, so you always know what a clip is worth before you make it.

There is no cap on what you can earn and no minimum you have to reach. A clip that does 5,000 views is paid, and a clip that does 5 million is paid at the same rate, on the same campaign.

Short vertical clips, on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Each campaign hands you the source material to work from, whether that is a trailer, gameplay, a stream or an announce clip, along with the brief: the angles that work, what is off-limits, and any embargo or credit rules the client set.

The editing is yours. A campaign is not a template to fill in, it is material and a set of rules, and the clip you build out of it is what makes the difference.

Views are verified before they are paid. Bots and boosted views are filtered out, on your clips and on everyone else's, which is what keeps the rates on this network where they are: our clients pay for verified views and nothing else, and that is what funds the payouts.

Your clips also have to match the brief. A clip that breaks the campaign rules comes down and earns nothing, and the rules are in the brief before you start, so nothing about that should ever be a surprise.

Most campaigns are open to every clipper in the network. Some are not: on the biggest launches, rights holders set conditions on who can post, and those campaigns open to selected clippers only. Selection is earned on what you have already delivered inside the network, not on your follower count. The app tells you which is which before you spend time on a clip.

Your earnings update in the app as views are verified, campaign by campaign and clip by clip, so you can see what you have made at any point in a campaign rather than at the end of it. You cash out from the app.
