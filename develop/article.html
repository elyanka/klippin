How to pick your first campaign

Every campaign card shows four things: the reward, the budget left, the time left and the brief. Here is how to read them in under a minute, and which campaigns to start with.

K Klippin teamClipper success

On this page

  1. Read the reward, not the budget
  2. Check what is left
  3. Mind the clock
  4. Read the brief twice
  5. Post, then watch

You have joined a factory and the campaign list is full. Some pay more per view, some have more budget, some end tonight. Picking well on day one matters more than clipping fast: a good first campaign gets you approved clips, verified views and your first payout.

Read the reward, not the budget

The reward is what you earn for every million verified views your clips bring in. It is the number that decides your pay. A big total budget only tells you how much the client has set aside for everyone.

  • Compare rewards across campaigns in the same vertical, not across gaming and music.
  • Check the platforms. A campaign that pays on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube lets one clip earn three times.
  • Look for a max per clip. Some campaigns cap what a single clip can earn.

Check what is left

The bar under the reward shows how much of the budget has been paid out. When it fills up, the campaign stops paying, even if your clip keeps getting views. Early in a campaign there is more room for everyone.

Tip

New to clipping? Start with a campaign that is under half paid out and has at least two days left. You get time to learn the brief without racing the budget.

Mind the clock

Time pills on the card tell you when a campaign starts or ends: Starts in 3h, 3h left, Ended 12.10. Views keep counting for a while after you post, so a clip posted in the last hour rarely has time to earn much.

“My best clips were never the fastest ones. They were the ones that followed the brief to the letter.”

@nightclips, top 10 in the Neon Drop campaign

Read the brief twice

The brief tells you what to clip, how long the clip can be and what has to be in it. A clip that misses one line is rejected, and rejected clips earn nothing.

  1. Use only the assets the campaign gives you.
  2. Keep to the length and format it asks for.
  3. Add the tags and the link it asks for, exactly as written.

Post, then watch

Submit your clip in the app as soon as it is live. Its views show up in your submissions, and they turn into earnings once they are verified. From there you can withdraw to your bank account.

That is it. Pick one campaign, read its brief twice, post a clip you are proud of, and see what the first week brings.
