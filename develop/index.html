How it works

Three steps. That’s the whole thing.

No application to write, no follower count to hit. The clips you already know how to make, pointed at campaigns that pay.

  1. Install the app

    Sign up in under a minute and connect the accounts you post from. Nothing to pay, nothing to prove upfront.

  2. Join a campaign

    Browse what’s live, pick the ones you want. Each campaign shows what it pays, what it needs, and the assets to clip from. Some are open to everyone, the biggest ones open on approval.

  3. Post and earn

    Publish on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Views are verified, then they count. Follow every clip and every dollar in the app, from the moment you post.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need any experience?

No. Plenty of the clippers in the network had never edited a video before they joined. What decides how far you go is whether you learn what works, and that is what the brief is for: every campaign tells you the angles that land, what is off-limits and what the client expects, before you make anything.

You also find out fast. Every clip is reviewed against the brief, so you know what worked and what did not instead of guessing.

Do I need a big following?

No. There is no follower minimum. You are paid per verified view, so what counts is how far each clip travels, not the size of the account it was posted from.

What do I need to get started?

The Klippin app and at least one account to post from on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. Signing up takes under a minute and costs nothing. Each campaign then gives you the brief and the assets to clip from.

How does the pay actually work?

Every campaign sets its own rate per verified view and shows it before you join, along with its budget and any cap on what a single clip can earn. Your earnings add up in the app as the views on your clips are verified.

What do I post, and where?

Short clips cut from the assets each campaign provides, made to its brief. You publish them on your own TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts accounts, then submit each clip in the app so its views can be tracked.

How are views counted?

Every clip you submit is reviewed against the brief and tracked from the moment it is published. Views are verified before they count toward your earnings, and you can follow the views and earnings of each clip in the app.

Why are some campaigns “on approval”?

The biggest campaigns choose who clips for them. You apply from the campaign in the app, and the work you have done on open campaigns is what they look at. Every other campaign is open to everyone, from day one.

When and how do I get paid?

Earnings show as pending until the views behind them are verified. Then they become available to withdraw, and you send them to your bank account from the wallet in the app.

Who am I actually clipping for?

Brands, artists, game studios and creators in entertainment, mostly gaming, esports and music. Each campaign shows who it is for, so you always know whose content you are clipping.
