Rules · Scope#

Rules

Written on 30.10 from what the kit and the 184 Full kit screens do today. When a screen and this page disagree, fix the screen or add a kit variant first.

Scope

What this round changes and how screens are put together.

What changes

  • Only how things look: colour, type, spacing, components and labels. Flows, screens and content stay as they are.
  • Nothing goes into the live app until the client approves it.

How screens are built

  • Every screen is assembled from kit components. A new need becomes a component or variant in the kit first, then goes on the screen.
  • Dark only. Colours, type, spacing and radii come from semantic tokens (bg/, text/, border/, brand/, status/), never raw values.
Rules · Surfaces & colour#

Surfaces & colour

Depth comes from fill steps. Colour carries meaning, so most of the screen stays neutral.

Four levels

  • Screen: bg/base.
  • Containers: cards and list rows on bg/surface with a 1px border/default, radius/md (12), 16 padding.
  • Elements inside a container: chips, filter chips, pills, selects, fields, secondary and icon buttons on bg/tint. Pressed or selected: bg/tint-strong, or the orange tint with an orange border for a selected filter.
  • Overlays: sheets, dialogs and menus on bg/raised with the overlay shadow, over the scrim. Elements inside keep bg/tint.

Text colours

  • Primary (white) for titles, names and values.
  • Secondary for supporting text. It is the most used text colour in the screens.
  • Tertiary for meta: dates, counts, labels above values, inactive tabs and nav.
  • Orange only for money the user earns, active states (tab, nav, selected filter) and text buttons.
  • Red only for errors and destructive actions. Green only for status and passed validation.

Rules · Status#

Status

Every state is a chip or a tag from the kit, never free text.

Lifecycle chips

  • CAPS label with a 12px icon on a tinted fill. Live and Ongoing use a dot instead of an icon.
  • Green = active, amber = waiting, red = a problem or a violation, grey = finished or inactive.
  • PAID and NEW are the only filled chips: brand gradient with dark text.

Membership tags

  • Sentence case with a 16px coloured icon in a neutral tag. They describe the user’s relation to a factory, not its lifecycle.
  • Joined green, Pending amber, Applied green check, Open white with an open lock, Private grey with a lock.

Validation

  • A passed check is a green filled check circle (password rules, verified fields). An error is red text with a red icon under the field.
  • Banners: info for notices, neutral for read-only notes, error for problems.

Rules · Money#

Money

Orange means money the user can earn or take. Everything else is white or grey.

Colour

  • Orange: rewards, prizes, the available balance, earned amounts in the history, your own leaderboard amount.
  • White: totals, stats and other people’s amounts.
  • Grey: zero, cancelled, withdrawals, pending rewards and the budget after the slash.
  • Never green or red for money.

Format

  • Euro prefix, comma thousands, dot decimals: €1,000 · €4,812.60.
  • Money in a list that comes in gets a plus: +€93. Money going out has no sign.
  • Budgets and caps use a slash: €1,800 / €6,000. Rates: €2.40 / 1M views.
  • Space Grotesk for money on cards and heroes (display/m to display/xl). In rows and labels money uses Inter semibold.

Rules · Type#

Type

Inter for the interface, Space Grotesk only for money and big numbers.

Hierarchy as used

  • title/l: the screen title in the app header.
  • title/s: card and section titles.
  • body/m medium: row titles, names, list items. The most used style.
  • body/m and body/s: descriptions and messages.
  • caption: meta, labels above values, timestamps.
  • Buttons, chips and tags have their own label styles. Never restyle them.

Rules · Actions#

Actions

One main action per screen; everything else steps down.

Buttons

  • Primary L is the screen’s main action, full width at the bottom. It stays visible but disabled (40%) until the form is valid.
  • Secondary L is the alternative next to or under it (Cancel, Details).
  • Primary M is used for one thing only: Connect, with the link icon, everywhere an account is connected.
  • Text M for See all and inline links.
  • Pressed: primary darker, secondary bg/tint-strong. The app has no hover or focus.

Icon buttons and remove

  • Toolbars and headers use secondary M icon buttons (filter, sort, search, more). Selected = orange tint.
  • Remove is a red bin with no box, in every list.
  • A destructive confirmation uses the Danger soft button, never the orange primary.

Rules · Icons & navigation#

Icons & navigation

Phosphor only, regular weight; the fill variant marks an active or completed state.

Sizes

  • 24 by default (headers, rows, nav inside the app).
  • 20 for trailing row actions and in L buttons.
  • 16 inline with text, in M buttons and tags.
  • 12 in chips. 32 in the bottom nav.

Navigation

  • Bottom nav: Dashboard (squares-four), Factories (stack), Chat (chat-circle-dots), Profile (user). Active = fill in orange, others tertiary.
  • Chat shows a small orange dot when there are unread messages, no count.
  • Back is caret-left. Rows that open something end with a grey caret-right. Expand and collapse use a grey caret-down, no circle.
