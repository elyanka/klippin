Foundations · Elevation#

Elevation

Four levels. Depth comes from fill steps, not from shadows; only level 3 casts a shadow.

Level 0 · bg/base
Level 1 · bg/surface + border/default Factory card
Gaming Music Joined
Level 2 · bg/tint (white 6%) for chips, pills, secondary & icon buttons on any surface. Pressed: bg/tint-strong.
Level 3 · bg/raised + border/default + elevation/overlay Join this factory? Popups, sheets, menus, dropdowns and the calendar. Always over bg/scrim. Elements inside overlays keep bg/tint; selected or pressed = bg/tint-strong.

Foundations · Effects#

Effects

Shadows are rare. Only overlays (level 3), white icons on media, and the web hover glow on primary buttons get one.

elevation/overlay Level 3 only: popups, sheets, menus, dropdowns, calendar. Always with bg/raised + border/default over bg/scrim.
elevation/icon-on-media White icons placed on thumbnails/photos (e.g. network icon). Use with a bottom fade.
